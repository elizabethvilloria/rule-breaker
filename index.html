Rule Breaker

Creatures and relics rise from the temple floor. The Oracle carves a new rule every 12 seconds. Smash only what the rule names.

  • Right smash: gold, and combos multiply it
  • Wrong smash: you lose a whole heart
  • The Oracle studies you: it speeds up when you're fast, shows you more of what fools you, and hides targets where you're slow
  • Let one escape: you lose a quarter heart
  • Skull bombs spark and flicker: never smash them. The blast costs 2 hearts.
  • The Oracle is an AI: it invents new laws every game and aims the next ones at your mistakes
The Oracle is powered by Claude. You'll be asked once to let it speak.
